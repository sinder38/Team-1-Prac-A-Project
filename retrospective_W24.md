Sprint 4 Retrospective

What Went Well

Most team members completed their assigned tasks.
Team members helped each other when problems came up.
More development work was completed during this sprint.
GitHub reviews and teamwork improved as the sprint went on.
The team continued making progress on the project.

What Did Not Go Well

Some members were still learning their new roles.
Some tasks could not be finished until other tasks were completed first.
A few GitHub issues and merge conflicts caused delays.
Team communication was not always clear.
Some updates were received later than expected.

Actions for Next Sprint

Give updates earlier.
Raise blockers as soon as possible.
Communicate more with team members.
Review work earlier to avoid last-minute problems.
Continue helping each other when support is needed.
