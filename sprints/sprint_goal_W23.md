Sprint Goal for Team 1A

The sprint goal for Team 1A is to adapt to the team workflow, complete the tasks based on each member’s assigned role, and test different roles each week so everyone can understand the overall market prediction process. The team should focus on building a clear and evidence-based workflow where each member contributes to the SPX, NDX, and IWM prediction using structured reasoning, AI comparison, market evidence, and human judgement.
By the end of the sprint, each team member should become familiar with their role, create the required files, commit their work through the correct GitHub workflow, and actively communicate progress through the team Discord. This sprint is also about helping the team practise role rotation, improve collaboration, and prepare for stronger workflow consistency in the upcoming weeks.

What “done” looks like this week

This week, “done” means each team member has understood their assigned role, read the required guidance such as the Stock Trader Almanac, created the necessary files for their role, and committed their work through a branch in the team GitHub repository. The work should show that members understand their responsibilities and are preparing for the upcoming market prediction tasks.
The team should also show active communication by giving updates, asking questions, and engaging in the team Discord. This will help prove that everyone is taking responsibility, adapting to the workflow, and preparing to contribute more clearly in the next sprint.
