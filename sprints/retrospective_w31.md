# Retrospective

## What went well?

- The team successfully completed most of the planned sprint tasks.
- The frontend, backend, and documentation continued to improve.
- Team members actively supported each other when technical issues arose.
- New replacement LLM models were identified after free models became unavailable.
- Communication between the Product Owner and Scrum Masters remained consistent throughout the sprint.
- Daily stand-ups and mid-week check-ins helped monitor team progress.
- The live dashboard and overall system became more polished and ready for the final presentation.

## What problems did we face?

- Some team members were initially unsure what task to work on.
- Several pull requests required additional changes before they could be merged.
- CI failures and merge conflicts delayed some development work.
- Changes to external free LLM models required the team to quickly adapt and update the project.
- A few tasks were submitted late, leaving less time for review and testing.
- Communication from some team members was delayed, making it harder to track progress.

## Learnings & Action Items

- Assign follow-up tasks earlier for members who complete their work.
- Submit pull requests earlier to allow enough time for review and revisions.
- Keep local branches up to date to reduce merge conflicts.
- Continue reporting blockers as early as possible.
- Avoid editing code directly on GitHub Web; use a local development environment instead.
- Keep documentation updated alongside development rather than waiting until the end.
- Continue supporting teammates when technical issues arise to improve overall team efficiency.
