# Sprint 6 Retrospective

- Sprint Goal, team roles, and Definition of Done were confirmed before development.

- Frontend and backend integration required additional testing.

## What we learned

- Always prepare backup options for external APIs and third-party services.
- A fault-tolerant pipeline helps reduce the impact of technical failures.
- Regular communication helps the team identify blockers earlier.
- Completing work earlier gives more time for testing and integration.

## What we will improve next sprint

- Share progress updates more regularly in the group chat.
- Be more active and take responsibility for assigned tasks.
- Complete work earlier in the sprint.
- Continue improving pipeline reliability.
- Spend more time testing integration before submission.
