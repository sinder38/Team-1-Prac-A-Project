What went well?
Team communication improved through regular check-ins.
More pull requests were submitted as the sprint progressed.
Team members responded to review comments and continued improving their work.
Documentation and project tracking were updated throughout the sprint.
Scrum Master followed up with developers to monitor progress and identify blockers.
Code reviews helped improve the quality of submitted work.

What problems did we face?
Some development tasks progressed slower than expected.
Several pull requests were still waiting for reviews.
The pipeline encountered a rate-limit issue during testing.
Some tasks required multiple review rounds before they could be approved.
The product backlog needed updating because it contained outdated or irrelevant information.
A few dependencies delayed progress until updates or reviews were completed.

Learnings & Action Items
Check in with team members earlier and follow up more regularly.
Request code reviews as soon as work is ready.
Keep the product backlog updated throughout the sprint.
Resolve blockers as early as possible instead of waiting until the end of the sprint.
Improve communication when tasks or reviews are delayed.
Continue monitoring dependencies to reduce waiting time between tasks.

What we will improve next sprint
Improve communication between team members.
Follow up on blockers earlier.
Request PR reviews sooner.
Keep the product backlog updated.
Complete tasks earlier in the sprint.
Monitor dependencies more closely.
