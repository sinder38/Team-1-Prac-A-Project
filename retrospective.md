Sprint 4 Retrospective

What Went Well

Successfully transitioned several roles from manual processes to automated agents and pipelines.
Almanac, Technical, and LLM Synthesis agents were developed and submitted with passing checks.
CI/CD integration and GitHub workflow improvements increased automation and reliability.
Team members continued supporting documentation, Figma, presentations, and Scrum activities alongside development work.
Safeguards such as data sanitization, timeout handling, and fallback mechanisms improved system stability.

What Did Not Go Well

Some team members were still adapting to new software-focused roles and responsibilities.
Verification of certain Almanac statistics and datasets required additional effort.
Team engagement and coordination were more challenging during the transition to the new project phase.
Several tasks remained dependent on outputs from other roles before final validation could be completed.

Actions for Next Sprint

Improve onboarding and documentation for new role responsibilities.
Verify data sources earlier to reduce delays in validation.
Continue strengthening team communication and Scrum participation.
Complete final integration testing once all agent outputs are available.
Resolve review feedback quickly to keep pull requests moving efficiently.
