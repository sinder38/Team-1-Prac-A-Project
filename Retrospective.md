Sprint Retrospective

What Went Well

Team members completed their assigned deliverables and submitted work on time.
Collaboration improved after moving presentation work to Google Slides.
GitHub workflow was maintained through regular PR reviews and merges.
Weekly reports, market analysis, and QA activities were completed successfully.
Team coordination helped keep sprint progress aligned with deadlines.

What Did Not Go Well

Some team members were unfamiliar with Figma and GitHub Actions, which slowed down collaboration.
Communication issues in Discord occasionally caused confusion and delays.
Some task requirements were initially misunderstood, creating dependency-related blockers.
Presentation preparation and content verification took longer than expected.
QA work could not be fully completed until final prediction and comparison outputs were available.

Actions for Next Sprint

Provide clearer guidance and documentation for Figma and GitHub workflows.
Improve communication channels and keep discussions in the correct Discord threads.
Clarify task requirements earlier to reduce misunderstandings.
Standardise week naming and file naming conventions across all outputs.
Finalise deliverables earlier where possible to reduce downstream dependencies and QA delays.
