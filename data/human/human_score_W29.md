## Wild Card Insight

The market's leadership problem cuts both ways this week. Technology has been the main engine of this rally, and it just posted its worst sector week (-5.48%) while NDX confirmed a high-confidence bearish technical setup. That's a genuine trend change, not noise. But the same concentration and the sharpness of the move raise the odds of an oversold bounce, especially since the Fed is still expected to hold rather than surprise hawkishly. Whether this becomes a deeper leg down or a sharp reflex rally likely hinges on how Technology specifically trades in the days around the Fed decision.
