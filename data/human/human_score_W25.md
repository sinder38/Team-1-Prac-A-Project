## Five-Dimension Judgement

### 1. Macro / News Weight — Score: 0

Treasury yields continued to drift lower while oil prices fell 9.83% during the week, both supportive developments for risk assets. Progress toward an Iran peace agreement also reduced geopolitical tensions and helped ease pressure on energy markets.

However, the US Dollar strengthened by 0.99% and the Federal Reserve shifted slightly hawkish despite maintaining rates at 3.50%-3.75%. While financial conditions improved in some areas, the stronger dollar and cautious Fed stance offset much of that benefit.

Macro conditions remain balanced and do not currently provide a strong directional signal.

### 2. Technical Structure — Score: +1

SPX closed at 7,501, NDX at 30,406 and IWM at 295.59. All three indices remain above both the 8 EMA and 21 EMA, with the 8 EMA continuing to hold above the 21 EMA.

SPX remains above key support at 7,238, while NDX and IWM maintain High confidence bullish structures. Unlike Week 24, where the market was still recovering, the technical picture is now clearly bullish across all three major indices.

### 3. Almanac Seasonal Weight — Score: -1

June remains the weakest month in the US midterm election cycle and continues to rank near the bottom of historical seasonal performance tables.

The market remains inside the Q2-Q3 Weak Spot and the Almanac Agent continues to classify the environment as bearish-neutral. Technology remains the primary seasonal exception, but broader sector seasonality remains negative.

We maintain the bearish seasonal score until the market moves beyond this historically weak period.

### 4. AI Model Agreement Quality — Score: +1

All four models — NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Super, OpenAI gpt-oss-120b, Google Gemma 4 31B and Poolside Laguna M.1 — independently returned an Uncertain or Neutral regime with Medium confidence.

All four models identified the same three core drivers: strong bullish technical momentum, persistent June seasonal weakness and a mixed macro environment. The quality of agreement remains high due to convergence on both direction and reasoning.

### 5. Wild Card / Human Observation — Score: +1

Technology continues to provide the majority of market leadership. XLK remains inside its seasonal long window while broader sectors such as Financials, Materials and Energy remain under seasonal pressure.

This creates an unusual market structure where a small number of leadership sectors are carrying broader index performance. None of the models fully emphasized this concentration risk. Continued technology leadership may allow markets to overcome seasonal weakness, while any loss of momentum could quickly expose the broader market to seasonal headwinds.
