### Best 3 this week
1. **Materials** — up 3.57%. Specialty chemicals and industrial inputs led (e.g. Linde +3%).
2. **Consumer staples** — up 2.77%. Defensive names held up — Coca-Cola, Walmart, Philip Morris all gained.
3. **Financials** — up 2.59%. Banks and asset managers rallied (Citigroup +6%, Capital One +7%, Blackstone +7%).

### Worst 3 this week
1. **Communication** — down 1.50%. Only sector in the red. Meta and Google dragged; telecom (Verizon +7%) couldn't offset.
2. **Energy** — up just 0.07%. Flat despite the broad rally — oil prices fell ~6% and Exxon/Chevron were weak.
3. **Utilities** — up 0.40%. Lagged the rest of the market in a risk-on week.

**In plain words:** A broad recovery — 10 of 11 sectors finished green. The standout story inside tech was a **chip rally**: Intel (+26%), KLA (+32%), Applied Materials (+25%), and Micron (+14%) soared, while software giants like Oracle (-14%), Microsoft (-6%), and Apple (-5%) still struggled. Financials and defensives joined the bounce; only communication services finished down.
