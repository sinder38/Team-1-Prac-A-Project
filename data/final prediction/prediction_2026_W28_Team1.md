## HUMAN OVERRIDE / WILD CARD

Our team gives greater weight to the Federal Reserve event than the AI models appear to assign. The current bullish setup depends heavily on continued technology leadership and stable Treasury yields.

If the Fed delivers a more hawkish message, rising yields could place immediate pressure on technology valuations. Because SPX and NDX are already close to resistance, even a small negative surprise could trigger profit-taking.

Technology leadership remains the key wild card. Continued strength would support a further move higher, but any loss of momentum could quickly weaken the broader market.
