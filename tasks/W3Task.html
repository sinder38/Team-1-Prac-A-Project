Weekend Sprint Wisdom

This week, you are doing manually what your future Prism tool should eventually help automate.

The weekend sprint is painful by design, but not pointless. Every manual step — gathering market evidence, comparing AI models, scoring human judgement, tagging a release, and defending your reasoning — is a clue for what your future agentic AI market-intelligence tool should automate.

Scrum exposes disorder early enough to fix it.
If your team feels confused, that is not necessarily failure. It is information.
Done means evidence exists.
If the work cannot be inspected in GitHub, Discord, FigJam, or the deck, it is not truly visible.
The release tag is your timestamped promise.
A prediction that can be edited after the result is known is not a prediction.
Retrospective is not blame.
It is the disciplined habit of turning frustration into the next process improvement.
